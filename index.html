About

Not only a developer.

Most portfolios show code. This one shows what the code is for — and what happens after it ships.

Ali started as a backend developer — multi-tenant architecture, serverless APIs on AWS, React and Next.js interfaces. That engineering discipline — trace the process, find where it breaks, design a fix, test it, ship it — never left.

Today he applies it to real business operations: running Odoo 18 across sales, inventory and purchasing for a footwear and apparel retailer, keeping 100,000+ items moving cleanly, and managing the Shopify storefronts and e-commerce operations that sell them.

That combination — software development and ERP/e-commerce operations — is the differentiator. He doesn't just build digital products; he understands the warehouse, the checkout, and the spreadsheet that connects them.
